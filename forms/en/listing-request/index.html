Request a listing

To list an MCP service on MCPNavi, send its details with this form. Listing is free.

About this form

MCPNavi is a Japanese directory of MCP services, arranged by what they are used for. Use this form to send the name of the service and the URLs of the public pages that state its details.

  • Listing is free.
  • Every detail on MCPNavi is read from a public page. What you enter in the form is never published as it is.
  • A detail that no public page states is shown as "Unknown".
  • Each service shows the date its details were last checked.

About the data explains what each field means and how it is checked. The FAQ covers the cost, how a service gets listed, and how to correct the listed details.

What the form asks for

  • Service: name, official page and summary, plus the URLs of the documentation, the repository and the pricing page
  • Provider: name, type and the URL of the company profile page
  • Japanese support: the URLs of the Japanese documentation and the Japanese support page
  • Contact: your name and email address (never published)

Fill in a URL field only when such a page exists. The more URLs you give, the fewer details are listed as "Unknown".

What happens next

  1. After you send the form, a confirmation page is shown. No confirmation email is sent.
  2. MCPNavi reads the public pages at the URLs you entered and checks the details for the listing.
  3. If anything needs clarification, MCPNavi contacts you at the email address you entered.
  4. When the check is done, the page for the service is published.

MCPNavi lists MCP services. A service may not be listed when it is not an MCP service, or when no public page states its details.
