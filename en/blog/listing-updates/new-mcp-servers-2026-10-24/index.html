New MCP server listings (October 2026)

We have added seven MCP servers for subsidy search, market data, SEO automation, job search and an AI training data marketplace.

I Iqbal Abdullah

Contents

  1. Services from Japan
  2. International services

We have added seven MCP servers to MCPNavi. One is from Japan and six are from overseas. All are remote servers: you register the URL and connect.

Services from Japan

補助・助成.jp

A remote server that searches national and local government subsidies and grants from an AI agent, checks eligibility against the official guidelines and fills in the application forms.

International services

OwlChart

A remote server that gives AI agents crypto and stock market data: open interest, liquidations, funding rates, spot ETF flows and chart patterns.

Aident Loadout

A remote server that gives AI agents Actions for connected apps and Skills through one connection. Aident Vault holds the credentials, and Audit records each Action.

Seonix MCP

A remote server that gives AI agents a Seonix project's site health, Search Console data, keyword clusters and articles. Available on paid Seonix plans.

NeverApply

A remote server for searching jobs, viewing matches and applications, tailoring resumes, preparing for interviews and applying to jobs you approve, from an AI agent.

RobotTraining.jobs

A remote server that searches open robot training jobs, such as teleoperation, demonstration data collection and autonomous vehicle and drone testing, with their hourly pay.

AIData.Broker

A remote server that gives AI agents published AI training data prices, market rates, buyers and their terms, licensing deals, and lawsuits and rulings, each with its source.

You can read the details of each service on its page.
