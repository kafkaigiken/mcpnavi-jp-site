More companies adopt MCP: Upwork and examples from Japan

More companies now let AI agents use their services through MCP. Upwork calls its MCP server a new acquisition channel, and in Japan, Mercari and Reiwa Travel have released MCP servers. Here is what each company did and why.

I Iqbal Abdullah

Contents

  1. MCP adoption in Japan and abroad
  2. Why companies adopt MCP
  3. Companies that help build apps
  4. What to watch
  5. Sources

More companies in Japan and abroad now let AI agents use their services, and the tool they use for it is MCP. An MCP server is a plugin that connects an AI application such as ChatGPT or Claude to an outside service. For how it works, see the introduction to MCP.

In Japan, 206 companies announced an MCP server of their own on PR TIMES in the first half of 2026. In the US, Upwork described its MCP server as a new customer acquisition channel in its quarterly results.

The reasons differ from company to company, and changes in search traffic are only one of them. This post sets out what each company did and why, from public sources.

MCP adoption in Japan and abroad

Japan: MCP announcements grew about 3.8 times in six months

MCPNavi studied the MCP services announced on PR TIMES from January to June 2026. 206 companies announced an MCP server of their own, in 365 releases. Releases per month grew from 29 in January to 109 in June, about 3.8 times. The number of companies announcing for the first time also rose, from 24 in January to 54 in June. For the details, see MCP services announced in the first half of 2026.

The largest group is business SaaS: inventory, HR, contracts and accounting. Money Forward, for example, announced a remote MCP server for its cloud accounting service in March. Other companies use an MCP server to let AI work with data they have collected themselves, such as Aucfan's market price data, SalesNow's company data and Halex's weather data.

Upwork: AI agents as a new kind of customer

Upwork is a marketplace that connects companies that need work done with freelancers. It released an MCP server in August 2026 and reported how it is used in its third-quarter results on 2 November. According to the results, the MCP server is "opening our marketplace to AI agents as a new class of client."

  • AI agents have made nearly 50 million requests since the launch.
  • Weekly active users are 20 times what they were at the launch in August.
  • Gross services volume (GSV) from work started through the MCP server grew more than 150% month over month in October.
  • Connects revenue through the MCP server passed an estimated annual run rate of $3 million. Connects are the virtual currency that freelancers use on Upwork to bid on projects and promote their services.

According to CEO Hayden Brown, clients can take actions such as drafting a job post or searching for talent "from inside any AI tool, like Claude or ChatGPT."

The run rate is $3 million, but that is about 0.4% of the annual rate of Upwork's third-quarter revenue of $185.6 million, so the channel is still small (MCPNavi's calculation).

Consumer services: apps inside ChatGPT

In 2026, several Japanese consumer services announced that people can now use them inside ChatGPT. They use OpenAI's Apps in ChatGPT, which lets a ChatGPT conversation call an outside service. OpenAI's developer documentation describes the MCP server as the way an app works with another service.

Start date Company What you can do in ChatGPT
9 March 2026 ZOZO Get outfit suggestions from WEAR and see item details from ZOZOTOWN
12 March 2026 Reiwa Travel (NEWT) Search overseas tours. Booking happens on the NEWT site.
23 June 2026 Mercari Search for items and draft a listing
17 September 2026 Money Forward Refer to household budget and asset data from Money Forward ME in the conversation

None of the four announcements says that a purchase or a booking can be completed inside ChatGPT. People look for options in the conversation, then finish on the company's own app or site.

Mercari and Reiwa Travel have also released MCP servers apart from their ChatGPT apps.

  • Mercari released Mercari MCP in January 2026. It is the connection layer that lets AI call Mercari's features, and the ChatGPT app is built on it.
  • Reiwa Travel released NEWT MCP on 11 June 2026. AI agents such as Claude can use it to search tours and hotels and to check availability and prices. The company says it aims to be "the infrastructure that connects AI agents and travel."

The company behind MCPNavi

Kafkai Giken, which runs MCPNavi, released Kafkai Ver.4 on 28 September 2026. Kafkai changed from a service you log in to and operate on screen into a data source that AI agents connect to over MCP. Users ask for Kafkai's data from the AI they already use, such as ChatGPT or Claude. MCPNavi lists it as Kafkai MCP.

Why companies adopt MCP

The announcements give four main reasons.

1. Search traffic is changing

Upwork named the change in search as a reason. CEO Hayden Brown said on the earnings call: "AI is also changing how customers find us."

Brown also said that "traditional search is being disrupted as more people turn to AI assistants for answers, recommendations, and actions," and that "we have been adapting our acquisition strategies to these changes."

Reddit also reported a fall in search traffic in its results at about the same time. According to its letter to shareholders, search traffic was down by more than one million visits a week on average in October.

In Japan too, fewer people go from search results to a website.

  • A study by Values: the share of Google searches that led to a site visit fell to 41.1% in 2025, as reported by Web Tantousha Forum.
  • Hakuhodo DY ONE's AI Search White Paper 2026: 23.9% of people ended a search without visiting a site, because an AI answer or a similar service gave them what they needed.

Being cited in an AI answer does not seem to bring many visits either. A study based on the browsing records of 900 US adults found that people clicked a source cited in Google's AI Overview only about 1% of the time. Kafkai Giken covered the study in AI Overview citations get about 1% of clicks. The sources that get cited are also uneven. Mr. Suzuki of the All Japan SEO Association said at a seminar that AI answers tend to draw on government bodies, universities, large companies and large portal sites (seminar report).

With an MCP server, an AI agent can call a company's service directly. That is a way in that does not depend on being cited in an AI answer.

Search is still a customer acquisition channel, though. In the Values study, 41.1% is the overall figure: for searches made while considering a purchase, the share that led to a site visit was 62.3%. AI answers are also built on top of search engines. The Kafkai Giken article LLMO and SEO: why AI search still depends on SEO points out that ChatGPT's search uses Bing results, and explains that SEO is still the base for any work aimed at AI.

2. Some needs are hard to put into words

None of the four Japanese consumer services names falling search traffic as a reason. What they name is a limit of search itself: some things are hard to find with keywords.

  • ZOZO: in its own survey, about 70% of people had a picture of what they wanted but could not put it into words, so they could not find it by search.
  • Mercari: many items are one of a kind and hard to find by keyword or filter.
  • Reiwa Travel: many people start thinking about a trip before they have chosen where to go or set any conditions.

In a conversation, a vague request can still be narrowed down to a few options.

3. People now ask AI for advice

Money Forward named a growing demand from people who want to ask AI about money. Kafkai Ver.4 also lets users ask for data from the AI they already use. If people get things done inside AI applications, services need to be usable there too.

4. One connection reaches several AI applications

Mercari is building a connection layer that lets many AI services call its features, not only ChatGPT. NEWT MCP is also meant for several AI agents, Claude among them. When one MCP server connects to several AI applications, a company does not need to build a separate integration for each.

Companies that help build apps

Some companies now help others adopt MCP and build ChatGPT apps. Nikkei Cross Trend reported that Dentsu Group and Hakuhodo have started services for businesses. In the same article, Satoru Yamamoto of Dentsu Digital said: "A new economic zone that starts from ChatGPT will form, and it will bring big changes to marketing."

On 7 April 2026, Horus AI started a service that builds apps for e-commerce sites, booking services and portal sites. The company explains: "The starting point where users look for products and services is spreading beyond search engines and site search to chat AI."

What to watch

  • The number of companies: releases per month grew about 3.8 times in the first half of 2026. The question is whether this growth continues in the second half.
  • The effect on revenue: even at Upwork, revenue through the MCP server is about 0.4% of total revenue. Future results will show how this share changes.
  • Purchases and bookings: the four Japanese consumer services still send people to their own site or app for the last step. The next stage is whether that step moves into the conversation.

In the announcement of mcpnavi.jp, we wrote: "people adopt AI agents when the agents are useful to them, not when someone tells them to." MCP servers are chosen the same way: by whether they let an AI agent do something useful for the person using it.

The hosting, transport and authentication of each MCP service are on its page in the MCP service listing.
