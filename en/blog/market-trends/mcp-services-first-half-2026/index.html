MCP services announced in the first half of 2026: sorting 365 PR TIMES releases into 22 categories

206 companies announced 225 MCP services on PR TIMES from January to June 2026. We sorted them into MCPNavi's 22 categories: business SaaS is about a fifth, and data owners are joining fast.

I Iqbal Abdullah

PR TIMES is a press release site run by PR TIMES Corp. Japanese companies use it to announce new services and features, and anyone can read them. That openness makes it one of the best places to see what is happening in the market.

As part of our efforts to make MCPNavi the main information source for MCP connectivity that power your AI agents, we searched PR TIMES for announcements of MCP services.

From January to June 2026, 206 companies announced an MCP server of their own on PR TIMES. They published 365 releases in total: 29 in January, 109 in June — a roughly 3.8× jump in six months.

An MCP server is a bridge that connects an AI application like ChatGPT or Claude to an outside service. For more on MCP itself, see the introduction to MCP.

This article merges the 365 releases into 225 services and sorts them into MCPNavi's 22 categories. Then it pulls out what the numbers suggest and shows examples from each category.

How we counted

We searched PR TIMES for releases containing "MCP", "MCP server" and "Model Context Protocol". The search returned 2,399 releases. A language model read each body and sorted it into one of four groups:

  • The company announces its own MCP server or MCP support.
  • The company provides MCP infrastructure or support.
  • The company provides an MCP client.
  • The release only mentions MCP.

This article covers the first group only: releases dated 1 January to 30 June 2026. There are 365 of them.

Many releases cover the same service, so we merged them by company and service. That gives 206 companies and 225 services. Each service goes in one category. When a service could fit two, we picked the closest.

Keep these points in mind:

  • A language model did the sorting. We did not re-read every release by hand, so treat the counts as approximate.
  • The date is the release date, not the launch date. Announcements of planned services and of development that has just started are included.
  • We looked at PR TIMES only. Services announced on other sites, or only on a company blog, are not included.
  • We have not confirmed the hosting, transport or authentication of any of these services. When MCPNavi lists a service, it reads those values from public pages.

What the numbers suggest

1. The largest category is business SaaS for daily work

"Productivity" has 49 services, 22% of the total. Inside it are inventory management, ERP, HR evaluation, contract drafting, booking, online store builders and RPA. None of MCPNavi's current categories fits these well — we created "Productivity" with calendars, notes and automation in mind. Whether business systems need their own category is an open question.

2. About eight in ten announcing companies are not listed on a stock exchange

Of the 206 companies, 161 are unlisted. 32 are on the Tokyo Stock Exchange and 11 on overseas markets. PR TIMES files 170 of the companies (83%) under "information and communication". Small and mid-sized SaaS companies are moving faster than the big ones.

3. Companies that hold data are offering it through MCP servers

At least 14 services are MCP servers that let an AI read data the company collected itself:

  • Aucfan: market price data
  • SalesNow: company data
  • QFINDR: search of Japanese companies
  • Cabocia: securities reports (EDINET DB), real estate (FUDOSAN DB), disaster risk (防災DB) and government policy (SEISAKU DB)
  • Halex: weather data
  • Location AI: people-flow data
  • Space Shift: satellite data
  • REVISIO and M Data: TV viewing data
  • IRBANK: financial data
  • DATAZORA: corporate data (KIJI)

For a company that sells data, an MCP server is a doorway for AI.

4. Databases, design and monitoring come from the Japan arms of foreign vendors

All 6 services in "Databases" and all 3 in "Design" were announced by the Japan arm of a foreign vendor: Oracle Japan, Teradata Japan, Snowflake, Elasticsearch, Confluent, Figma, Miro and Autodesk. In "Monitoring and observability", 3 of the 6 are Datadog, New Relic and Grafana Labs. Japanese companies are most active where they have their own SaaS to plug in.

5. 17 releases claim to be the first in Japan or in their industry

The claims cover very narrow segments:

  • Payment processing (SP.LINKS)
  • IDaaS (GMO Trust Login)
  • Booking and scheduling (Jicoo)
  • Custom merchandise (SUZURI)
  • Online store builders (Color Me Shop)
  • AEO tools (Answer IO)
  • Meeting minutes (Rimo)
  • PC logs (Sevensense Marketing)

Each "first" is the company's own claim — MCPNavi has not confirmed it. Still, even a narrow segment now has its first MCP server.

6. LINE marketing tools form a distinctly Japanese group

Tools that send and manage LINE messages can now be driven by AI: L Message ("エルメ") from Michona, "Kantan LINE Step" from a vision, and Subscline. LINE-based tools are a distinctly Japanese feature of the MCP market.

7. The number of releases is not the number of services

The 365 releases cover 225 services because the same service is announced many times. The most frequent companies are ELSOUL LABO (SLV, 15 releases), Workato (14), Boomi (11) and Terrasky (10). Those four alone account for 50 releases, 14% of the total. Counting releases rather than services makes the market look bigger than it is.

8. Three categories have no announcements

"Version control", "Files and storage" and "Browser automation" have no PR TIMES announcements. MCPNavi lists general-purpose servers in these areas from foreign providers — GitHub MCP Server, Filesystem MCP Server and Playwright MCP. Japanese companies start by connecting their own products and data, not by releasing general-purpose servers.

We also counted names in titles: 21 releases name "Claude", 11 name "ChatGPT" and 4 name "Copilot". This counts only a name in a title. It does not tell you which client can use the service — that depends on hosting, transport and authentication together. For details, read remote and local MCP servers and MCP server authorization.
