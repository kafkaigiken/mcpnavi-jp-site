Iqbal Abdullah

CEO, Kafkai Giken, Inc.

Leads Kafkai Giken, Inc. from Tokyo. He has spent over two decades working in software engineering, product development and community building.

Originally from Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, he came to Japan in 1997 and graduated from the Faculty of Engineering of Saga University with a degree in Information Science. After engineering and business roles at Yahoo! Japan and Amazon Japan, he founded Xoxzo Inc., a telephony SaaS platform; Accrete Inc. acquired the company in 2021.

He is proficient in C and has deep expertise in Python and the Django framework. He is a Fellow of the Python Software Foundation (PSF), a co-founder of PyCon JP and PyCon MY, and the founder and representative of the Python Asia Organization. He received the 2024 PSF Community Service Award, and holds six patents covering technologies from authentication to telephony systems.
