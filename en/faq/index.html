Common questions about listing a service on MCPNavi and about correcting the listed details. For a question that is not answered here, write to info@mcpnavi.jp.

Listing a service

Q1.Does a listing cost anything?

A listing is free at present. None of the following costs anything:

  • An initial fee or a handling fee when you send a listing request
  • A fee while the service is listed, or for renewing a listing
  • A fee for correcting the listed details

There are no paid plans or options at present. If MCPNavi offers a paid service in the future, it states the content and the price in advance. Nothing is charged unless the provider applies for it (Terms of Service, Article 3).

Q2.Do we need to sign a contract?

No. When you send the listing request form, the form asks you to agree to the Terms of Service and the other conditions it lists.

Q3.How does a service get listed?

  1. Send the name of the service and the URLs of the public pages that state its details with the listing request form.
  2. MCPNavi reads the public pages and checks the details for the listing. If anything needs clarification, MCPNavi writes to the email address entered in the form.
  3. When the check is done, the page for the service is published.

MCPNavi decides whether to list a service. MCPNavi does not promise how long this takes.

Q4.Can a request be declined?

Yes. A service may not be listed when it is not an MCP service, or when no public page states its details. MCPNavi may not give the reason (Terms of Service, Article 6).

Q5.How long does a listing last?

At present a listing has no end date and needs no renewal. MCPNavi may change the listed details or end a listing.

Q6.Is what we enter in the form published as it is?

No. Every detail on MCPNavi is read from a public page. MCPNavi uses the URLs in the form to know which pages to read. A detail that no public page states is shown as "Unknown".

Q7.How is the email address in the form used?

MCPNavi writes to it if anything about the listing needs clarification. The address is also added to the MCPNavi mailing list for announcements. Your name and email address are never published. Personal information is handled under the privacy policy of Kafkai Giken, Inc.

Q8.Can we show on our own site that the service is listed?

Yes. On the service's page, follow the "Listing badge" link to see the badge images and the HTML to paste. The badge shows only that the service is listed on MCPNavi.

Correcting the listed details

Q9.How do we correct the listed details?

The listed details are read from the provider's public pages. First, update your public pages: the official site, the documentation, the repository and so on. MCPNavi applies the change the next time it checks them. MCPNavi decides when and how often it checks.

To tell MCPNavi about a change, send these three things to info@mcpnavi.jp:

  1. The URL of the service's page on MCPNavi
  2. The item to correct (for example, the authentication method)
  3. The URL of the public page that states the correct content

When several items change, put them all in one email.

Q10.Is every correction applied?

Not always. MCPNavi lists only what it can confirm on a public page, so content that no public page states yet is not listed. MCPNavi decides whether and when to apply a correction, and may not reply to each request individually (Terms of Service, Article 6).

Q11.Some items show "Unknown". Why?

No public page stated that item when MCPNavi checked. Write it on a public page, and MCPNavi applies it the next time it checks. About the data explains what each item means and how it is checked.

Q12.How do we ask MCPNavi to remove a listing?

Send the URL of the service's page and the reason to info@mcpnavi.jp. MCPNavi reads the request and decides what to do.

Using the site

Q13.Does MCPNavi recommend the listed services?

No. A listing does not mean that MCPNavi recommends a service, or guarantees its quality or its performance (Terms of Service, Article 3). Before you adopt a service, check its details on the provider's official site.

Q14.What is the "Last checked" date?

The day MCPNavi read the service's details from its public pages. Each service shows one. The provider may have changed the specification or the price after that date.

Q15.Does it cost anything to use MCPNavi?

No. Browsing the site needs no registration and no payment.
